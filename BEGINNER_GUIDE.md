# Beginner's Guide: How to Run Your Daily Habit Tracker

## What is this?

This is a **Weekly Habit Tracker** - a simple web application that helps you track your daily activities for:
- Voice exercises (Mini Activation, Articulation, Vocal Sessions)
- Sport activities (Cardio, Weights + Cardio)
- Work (Deep work blocks)
- Guitar practice (rated 0-5)

The app shows you weekly grids, calculates your progress percentages, and stores all your data locally in your browser.

---

## How to Run It (3 Easy Methods)

### Method 1: Double-Click (Easiest!)

1. **Find the file** called `index.html` in your project folder
2. **Double-click** on `index.html`
3. Your default web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, etc.) should open automatically
4. You're done! The app is now running in your browser

### Method 2: Right-Click and Open With

1. **Find** the `index.html` file
2. **Right-click** on it
3. Select **"Open With"** from the menu
4. Choose your preferred browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc.)
5. The app will open in that browser

### Method 3: Drag and Drop

1. **Open your web browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc.)
2. **Find** the `index.html` file in your file explorer/finder
3. **Drag** the file into the browser window
4. **Drop** it, and the app will open

---

## What You'll See

When the app opens, you'll see:

1. **Top Navigation Bar** with buttons:
   - `◀` and `▶` - Navigate between weeks
   - `+ New Week` - Create a new week
   - `Targets` - Set your weekly goals
   - `History` - View all your past weeks
   - `Overall` - See all-time statistics
   - `⚙` (gear icon) - Change default settings

2. **Weekly Grid** showing:
   - Rows for Voice, Sport, Work, and Guitar
   - Columns for each day (Mon-Sun)
   - Your progress for each activity

3. **Week Summary** showing:
   - Overall percentage
   - Detailed stats for each category

---

## How to Use It

### Logging Your Daily Activities

1. **Click on any day cell** in the weekly grid
2. A side panel will open on the right
3. **Check/select your activities** for that day:
   - Voice: Check boxes for activities you completed
   - Sport: Choose None, Cardio, or Weights + Cardio
   - Work: Use + and - buttons to count deep work blocks
   - Guitar: Click a number from 0-5 to rate your practice
4. Changes are **saved automatically**!
5. Close the panel by clicking the **×** button

### Viewing Your Progress

- The weekly grid shows your progress at a glance
- Green numbers = good progress
- Orange numbers = partial progress
- Gray numbers = no activity
- The summary below shows detailed percentages

### Saving Your Data

Your data is **automatically saved** in your browser's storage. You don't need to click "Save"!

**Important**: To back up your data:
1. Scroll to the bottom of the page
2. Click **"Export JSON"** to download a backup file
3. To restore, click **"Import JSON"** and select your backup file

## Troubleshooting

**Q: The file won't open when I double-click it**
- Try Method 2 or 3 above
- Make sure you have a web browser installed

**Q: I don't see any data**
- This is normal for first-time use!
- Click on a day cell to start adding your activities

**Q: My data disappeared**
- Check if you're using the same browser
- Data is stored per-browser, so switching browsers will show empty data
- Always export backups regularly!

**Q: Can I use this on my phone?**
- Yes! The app is responsive and works on mobile browsers
- Just open the `index.html` file in your mobile browser

## Tips for Beginners

1. **Start by setting your targets**: Click the `Targets` button to set weekly goals
